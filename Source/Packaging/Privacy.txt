SubWake: what it does and what it changes
=========================================

SubWake keeps a subwoofer with auto-standby awake. Every few minutes, while no other
audio is playing, it plays a short, quiet low-frequency tone through the output you
choose. You set the volume yourself.

What it changes on this PC
- Installs into your user folder (%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\SubWake). No admin rights.
- Saves its settings and a log in %LOCALAPPDATA%\SubWake.
- If you leave "Start SubWake when I sign in" checked, it adds itself to your
  sign-in apps (you can turn this off in the app or in Windows Settings).

Privacy
- SubWake collects no personal data and has no telemetry or tracking.
- It reads audio levels and which apps are recording only on this PC, to decide
  when to play. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.
- Its only network request is the optional update check: once a day it asks
  GitHub (api.github.com) for the newest SubWake release. Like any web request,
  GitHub sees your IP address. Nothing else is sent. You can turn it off on the
  next page or at any time in the app (Options > Check for updates).
- It never downloads or installs anything by itself.

Uninstall
- Settings > Apps > Installed apps > SubWake > Uninstall.
- Your settings and log stay in %LOCALAPPDATA%\SubWake. Delete that folder to
  remove them too.
