        Calibrate volume

        1. Set Windows, your audio interface or amp, and the sub to your usual volume.
        2. Close anything that plays sound. SubWake waits 16 minutes so the sub can fall asleep, and starts the wait over if it hears audio.
        3. Bursts then get 3 dB louder every ~14 s, up to −6 dBFS. Click Sub woke up the moment it does.
        4. You get a suggested volume. Your current volume only changes if you click Use.
